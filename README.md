# IronMath

Year 8 maths practice app (installable PWA), aligned to the school Scheme of Work, no calculator.

- Units 1-4 (autumn test): Number, Powers & Primes, Area & Volume, Pythagoras - 25 questions each.
- Unit 5 (after half term): Fractions & % - 15 questions.
- Endless generated practice for every unit, plus "Later in Year 8" extra practice.
- XP, streaks, badges, four themes (Settings > Look). Works offline. Progress stays on the device.

Open the site on a phone and choose "Add to Home Screen" / "Install app". Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step.
